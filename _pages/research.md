My research develops practical cryptographic and privacy-enhancing technologies for trustworthy digital systems. I work across foundational cryptography and applied security, with a particular focus on systems that can be deployed in blockchain, digital finance, cloud, and privacy-sensitive data-sharing environments.

<div class="research-card">
  <h2>Cryptography and Privacy-Enhancing Technologies</h2>

  <p>
    I design and analyse cryptographic primitives that provide strong security guarantees under realistic threats, including leakage, related-key attacks, malicious participants, and post-compromise exposure. My work covers public-key encryption, digital signatures, identity-based encryption, ring signatures, anonymous credentials, and zero-knowledge proofs.
  </p>

  <h3>Selected directions</h3>

  <ul>
    <li>Digital signatures, threshold signatures, multi-signatures, and ring signatures</li>
    <li>Anonymous credentials and privacy-preserving authentication</li>
    <li>Efficient zero-knowledge proofs and verifiable computation</li>
    <li>Leakage-resilient, related-key-secure, and non-malleable cryptography</li>
  </ul>

<div class="research-card">
  <h2>Privacy-Preserving Data Systems</h2>

  <p>
    I develop privacy-preserving protocols that enable data to be searched, shared, authenticated, and analysed without unnecessarily exposing sensitive information. These systems are motivated by practical applications in cloud computing, health, contact tracing, digital identity, and secure data collaboration.
  </p>

  <h3>Selected directions</h3>

  <ul>
    <li>Private set intersection and privacy-preserving data matching</li>
    <li>Secure data provenance and selective disclosure</li>
    <li>Searchable encryption and encrypted data access</li>
    <li>Privacy-preserving protocols for cloud and health-data applications</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Blockchain Security and Digital Finance</h2>

  <p>

My research includes confidential transactions, privacy-preserving payments, atomic swaps, payment channels, blockchain rewriting, anti-money-laundering analysis, and central-bank digital currency-related technologies.
  </p>

  <h3>Selected directions</h3>
