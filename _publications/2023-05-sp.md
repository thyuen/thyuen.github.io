BlindHub is the first Bitcoin-compatible privacy-preserving payment-channel hub supporting variable payment amounts while maintaining relationship anonymity. It introduces BlindChannel, blind adaptor signatures, and flexible blind conditional signatures to support practical private conditional payments.
