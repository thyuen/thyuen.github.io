This paper introduces a post-challenge auxiliary-input model for leakage-resilient public-key and identity-based encryption. The model captures leakage from both encryptors and decryptors after a challenge ciphertext is issued, addressing threats relevant to cloud applications and wireless sensor networks.

**Best Paper Award, ESORICS 2014.**
