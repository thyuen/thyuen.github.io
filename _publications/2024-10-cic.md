This work gives dual-form exponent-inversion Boneh–Boyen signatures and Gentry IBE under the symmetric external Diffie–Hellman (SXDH) assumption. It further constructs P-signatures for anonymous credentials and an accountable-authority IBE scheme from the same static assumption.
