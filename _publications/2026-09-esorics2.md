HedgeSwap is a universal atomic-swap protocol designed to resist griefing attacks, where one party aborts and locks the other party’s assets. It compensates compliant parties through a premium mechanism and addresses timeout-race and timeout-overlap challenges without relying on smart-contract functionality beyond signature verification.
