This work presents an online-friendly two-party ECDSA protocol with a lightweight online phase and only one multiplicative-to-additive conversion in the offline phase. Its implementation improves communication and computation costs over previous online-friendly approaches and extends naturally to 2-out-of-\(n\) threshold ECDSA.
